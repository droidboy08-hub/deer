4 · Keys

Local keys act while the panel has focus. Page keys do nothing under it.

Anywhere

Ctrl+HOpen or close History; key repeat is ignored. From the address field, the typed text becomes its search. Page-first, because Docs and Word use it for Replace: F6 then Ctrl+H, or + › History, can’t be blocked.

In the panel

Up, DownMove by row; day headings are stops
Home, EndFirst or last loaded row; reaching the end loads more
Page Up, Page DownOne screen, 13 rows
Ctrl+Up, Ctrl+DownPrevious or next day heading
EnterOpen here; the panel closes
Alt+EnterA new tab, switched to
Ctrl+EnterA background tab; the panel stays
Shift+Enter, Ctrl+QPeek
SpaceToggle the selection; on a heading, the whole day
Shift+arrowsExtend the selection from the anchor; Shift+Home and Shift+End too
Ctrl+ASelect every match, in search results only
Delete, Shift+DeleteThe selection, or else the focused row, that day’s visits only; on a heading, the day. In Recently closed, the entry.
Ctrl+ZUndo the last delete while its line shows; not in the search field
Ctrl+CCopy the address, or the selection’s, one per line
Shift+F10, Menu keyThe row’s or heading’s menu, 4 px below it
Ctrl+FFocus search and select its text
Tab, Shift+TabSearch → views → Clear browsing data… → toolbar → list, one stop → Close. Focus stays inside.
Ctrl+WClose the panel, never the tab

Opening a row

Click, EnterOpens here: the focused side in split view, or in place of Home. The panel closes; focus goes to the page.
Ctrl+click, middle-click, Ctrl+EnterA background tab, placed as Settings › Tabs says; the row gets the wash. The panel stays.
Ctrl+Shift+click, Alt+EnterA new tab, switched to. The panel closes.
Shift+click, Shift+Enter, Ctrl+QA peek. History steps aside, and returns when the peek closes.

Double-click and Alt+click act as a click. Opening any page commits a pending delete first; a file: row has no Peek.

Esc, one step per keydown

The top of the stack goes first; each press takes one step.

  1. FirstMenuAn open row or heading menu closes
  2. ThenSelectionCleared; the rows stay
  3. ThenSearch textCleared, while focus is in the field
  4. LastPanelCloses; focus returns to where it was, or to the page

The house order stays: panel, then peek, then tab.

Window keys still act underneath: Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+1–9 and Ctrl+Shift+T switch or reopen tabs, and the panel keeps focus.

5 · Motion and edge cases

Motion

One spring, cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1); expand cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
Open280 ms on the spring, as Settings: the sheet 0.96 → 1; the scrim 0 → 0.3 in 200 ms
Close140 ms: the sheet 1 → 0.985 and fades; the lens goes first
Panel hand-offThe scrim holds at 0.3; one sheet closes in 140 ms as the other opens
HoverThe plate at once; favicon to checkbox and × in, 120 ms cross-fades
Selection modeSlots cross-fade, 120 ms; heading text slides to the titles, 240 ms on the spring
Delete a row100 ms fade, then a 240 ms collapse on the spring; the rows below slide up
Delete a day160 ms fade, then a 280 ms collapse on the spring
Undo240 ms expand on the spring; the content fades in from 80 ms, over 160 ms
Peek hand-offHistory out in 140 ms · the sheet grows from the row, 400 ms · back into the row, 280 ms, as History returns
WashThe accent wash on a row fades over 900 ms
JumpsCtrl+Up, Ctrl+Down, Home and End are instant, never smoothed
Reduced motionEvery moment becomes a 150 ms cross-fade: no scale, collapse, slide or wash travel

Edge cases

Deleting a rowRemoves that page’s visits on that day only; it stays under its other days and in the address field
Pending deleteCommitted when the undo line goes, on another delete, when the panel closes, when a page opens from it, or when Clear browsing data runs
Crash before the commitThe pages stay in history; the owner accepted this
A page visited againIts time updates in place; rows never jump under the pointer
MidnightHeadings relabel in place: Today becomes Yesterday
Split viewOpen here loads into the focused side; new tabs open after the pair
Popup windowsThe panel opens in the most recent normal window of the same privacy
Bookmarked pagesKeep the bookmark; that day’s visits and the row go
Daylight savingDays of 23 or 25 hours; every boundary is a local midnight
Private windowsNothing is shown, not even Recently closed: one line, “History isn’t shown in private windows.” Ctrl+H and + › History still open it.
