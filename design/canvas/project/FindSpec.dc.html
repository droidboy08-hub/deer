Find in page: motion and keys

The active tab pill becomes the find field in place. Nothing else in the bar moves, and the page keeps Chromium's own highlights.

1 · Ctrl+F The pill turns into the find field in place: Back and Forward fade out, reload turns into ×, and the favicon slides left, 240 ms, spring. The field has focus at once, so typing never waits.
2 · Next match Chromium jumps only when the match is off-screen, never smoothed. The count rolls, 160 ms. The ring lands on the match: it closes in from 16 px out, holds, then fades, 780 ms in all.
3 · Esc Find closes in 200 ms. The match stays selected and takes focus, or its link does, so Enter follows the link and Shift+Enter peeks it. The reload slot ignores clicks for 400 ms.
4 · Tab bar hidden Only the pill drops in, 300 ms. It stays while find is open. On close the address shows for 400 ms, then rises.
Keys
Ctrl+FOpen find with the selection or the last query, selected. Again selects all. Enter, Shift+EnterNext and previous match. Also F3, Shift+F3, Ctrl+G and Ctrl+Shift+G. Ctrl+EnterClose find and click the match. Ctrl+QClose find and peek the match's link. Alt+CMatch case. Ignored with Ctrl, so AltGr+C still types. Up, Down, Page Up, Page DownScroll the page without leaving the field. F6Move to the page, find parked. F6 from the page comes back. EscClose find, keeping the match selected. Esc unwinds one layer at a time.
Edge cases
Page keeps Ctrl+FThe page gets find keys first. F6, then Ctrl+F, always reaches Vitre's find. Still loadingThe counter stays blank until something is found. Find runs once more when loading stops, unless the user has stepped or scrolled. Match under the glassThe page scrolls at once so the match sits at y 92. On fixed or sticky content the pill drops to its parked tint instead. Leaving the pageAnother document closes find and keeps the query. A link within the page keeps find open. Typing with an IMESearch waits until the composition ends, and the counter dims. PDFThe viewer paints its own highlights and counts page by page. No ticks, guard or pre-fill. Thousands of matches“1 of 4,812+” while Chromium counts. The field never narrows below 160 px.
