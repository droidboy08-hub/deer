Left focused The outlined half; its host at weight 500. The whole pill takes this side’s glass: light, in the mixed tint because the other page is dark.
Right focused The plate glides between the halves, 240 ms on the spring, and the whole pill turns to this side’s dark glass. Here the right side is loading: a line under its half, and Stop in place of Reload.
Hover A plate and a × on that half. The × closes that side; its tooltip is “Close this side”.
Home side Focused, so the whole bar takes clear glass, in the mixed tint because the page beside it is light. “Search” in the placeholder colour; typing loads the page into that side.
Narrow The pill gives way to 360 and the halves to 110 each before any circle shrinks.
Background pair A 96×44 capsule, the footprint of two circles: at rest, hovering the right half (its badge closes that side), and compact at 64×30. Click a favicon to show the pair with that side focused.
Find over a pair One surface in the mixed tint, searching the focused side.

3 · Menus

Below a 648 px window every split row greys out: “Window too narrow”.

Split pill, on a half: acts on that side. Ctrl+W only on the focused half.

Pair capsule: the background pair. Close both tabs closes the two sides.

Tab circle, while a pair is active: replaces the unfocused side, which returns as a circle.

Link, in a pair: loads into the other side; Back returns.
