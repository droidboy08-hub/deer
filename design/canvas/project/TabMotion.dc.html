Tab switcher motion

Timings and curves for building the full-screen switcher. Every move in the deck uses one spring, so it behaves like one object; only the final expand uses its own curve.

1 · Press Ctrl+Tab The page you were on shrinks into its card at the left of the deck, 420 ms, spring. Your wallpaper blurs in behind it, 0 → 44 px, 320 ms.
2 · The deck settles The previous tab rises into the centre already selected; the others follow 60 ms apart, 560 ms each. The tab dock rises 18 px, 380 ms, starting at 140 ms.
3 · Tab again The deck slides one card, 560 ms, spring. The new card grows 0.86 → 1; the old one dims to 50%. The dock pill moves and widens 40 → 216 px, 420 ms.
4 · Release Ctrl The chosen card fills the window: scale 1 → 1.667, corners 22 → 0 px, 360 ms. The other cards and the dock fade out, 220 ms.
Curves
Springcubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)Shrinking, sliding, the dock and closing gaps. Fast start, soft landing.
Expandcubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)Only for the chosen card filling the window on release.
Other moments
Close a tabCtrl+W or Delete, or middle-click a card. The card lifts 12 px and fades, 220 ms; the neighbours close the gap with the spring. CancelStep back to the card you started on and let go, or click the wallpaper: the deck reverses, 360 ms. Esc can't cancel while Ctrl is held, because Ctrl+Esc opens Start. Quick tapCtrl+Tab released within 150 ms switches straight to the previous tab; the deck never appears. Reduced motionFollows the Windows animation setting: cross-fades only, 150 ms, no scaling or sliding. PerformanceCards move with transform and opacity only. Thumbnails are captured when you leave a tab, so the deck never renders live pages.
