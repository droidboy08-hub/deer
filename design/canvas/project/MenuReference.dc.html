Every menu

Every menu Vitre shows, at 1:1. Rows that only apply sometimes are noted under each menu.

Pages, links and images
Page264 × 302
Right-click the page, or a javascript: link.
Back and Forward grey out; they are never removed.
Link264 × 234
Right-click a link. Selected text inside it adds
Copy, Ctrl+C, above Copy link address.
Link to a file264 × 123
A link to a file keeps only Download linked file
and Copy link address.
Email link264 × 89
A mailto: link. A tel: link reads
Copy phone number instead.
Image264 × 234
Right-click an image that is not a link.
Inside a peek, Peek image is left out.
Canvas264 × 123
A canvas element. It can be saved and copied, but
has no address to open, peek or copy.
Image that is a link264 × 345
The link rows, then the image rows without
Peek image and Copy image address.
Media
Video280 × 302
Pause and Mute swap to Play and Unmute. Copy video
address is left out for blob: and MSE sources.
Protected video264 × 268
DRM video. Download video… greys out and says
Protected. MSE sources have no address to copy.
Audio264 × 268
Audio, with Loop on. Check items show a check
in the icon column and close the menu.
Peek
Peek page264 × 311
The page inside a peek, starting with Open as tab.
Back and Forward grey out until it has history.
Peek header264 × 157
Right-click the peek’s header.
Link in a peek264 × 200
A link inside a peek. Peeks don’t nest,
so Peek link is left out.
Home
Home wallpaper264 × 166
Paste and go needs text on the clipboard; a
video wallpaper adds Pause background.
Tab bar and downloads
Tab pill264 × 234
The active tab pill. Paste and go needs text on
the clipboard; Mute tab, a tab that made sound.
Address being edited264 × 225
The pill while you edit the address.
History suggestion304 × 46
A history row you moved to in the suggestions.
Background tab264 × 262
A background tab. The caption names it. Close tab
has no key: Ctrl+W closes the active tab.
New tab button288 × 191
The + circle. Reopen names what comes back (tab,
peek or window) and greys out when there is none.
Downloads ring264 × 191
Pause all becomes Resume all when paused. Download
copied link needs a URL on the clipboard.
Text and editing
Selected text320 × 166
Selected text, quoted up to 24 characters.
Search for opens the results in a Peek.
Selected address328 × 166
A selection that is a URL or a domain shows
Go to in place of Search for.
Text field304 × 302
A text field. Paste as plain text shows only
when the clipboard holds rich text.
Password field264 × 268
A password field: Cut and Copy always grey out.
Misspelled word264 × 336
A misspelled word, here “flaot”. Up to three
suggestions in Semibold, then Add to dictionary.
Find on page
Find field264 × 268
The find field in the tab pill: the editing rows
plus Match case, and no Inspect.
