Peek motion and keys

A link opens in a glass sheet without leaving the page. Every move starts or ends at the link you clicked, and the tab bar is where a peek becomes a tab.

1 · Shift+click a link The sheet grows out of the link, 400 ms, spring. The page dims to 22% and the tab bar stays visible. A frosted cover fills the sheet until the page paints, then fades, 200 ms.
2 · Hop to another link Shift+click a link still visible on the dimmed page. The sheet stays where it is and its content cross-fades, 200 ms.
3 · Close Esc, a click on the dimmed page or Ctrl+W. The sheet shrinks back into its link, 280 ms, and the link glows for 900 ms so you know where you were; keyboard focus returns to it.
4 · Open as tab Alt+Enter, the Open as tab button or a double-click on the header. The sheet fills the window, 380 ms. Its header rises into the tab bar as the new tab, right of its source, as the tabs re-flow, 420 ms.
Keys
Shift+click, Shift+EnterPeek a link. Shift+Enter in the address pill peeks a result. Ctrl+QPeek the link in focus, or the one under the pointer. Alt+EnterOpen the peek as a tab. Nothing reloads. EscClose. The page gets Esc first; press it twice within 400 ms to close anyway. After typing in the peek, that only nudges the sheet. Ctrl+WClose the peek, never the tab under it. Ctrl+Shift+TReopen a peek closed in the last 60 s, while its tab is active. Alt+LeftBack inside the peek; on its first page, closes it.
Edge cases
Link is a downloadThe sheet folds into a file-type circle that flies to the downloads button. Permission requestThe prompt hangs from the peek's header, not the window. Typed into the peekA click on the dimmed page only nudges the sheet, so nothing typed is lost. Small windowBelow 900 × 600 the sheet fills the window with an 8 px margin. On the home pageShift+Enter in the pill peeks over the wallpaper; Open as tab replaces Home. Full-screen videoFills the window without becoming a tab, then settles back into the sheet. Closed by accidentThe page stays loaded for 60 s, so peeking it again is instant.
