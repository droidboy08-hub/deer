Float glass

Field Notes · 6 min read

Most flat glass made today is float glass. Molten glass is poured onto a bath of molten tin, where surface tension pulls it into a sheet of even thickness. The glass counter page explains how long the ribbon takes to cool.

The process was developed by Pilkington in the 1950s. Before that, plate Glass had to be ground and polished on both sides. Coated Glass is made on the same line.

Because the tin is perfectly flat, the glass is too. The ribbon leaves the bath at about 600 degrees.
