Float glass

Field Notes · 6 min read

Most flat glass made today is float glass. Molten glass is poured onto a bath of molten tin, where surface tension pulls it into a sheet of even thickness. The counter page explains how long the ribbon takes to cool. The reference lives at refract.wiki for now.

The process was developed by Pilkington in the 1950s. Read the long history of the float process and of the people who made it work at scale, or write to the editors, call the desk, get the data set or the notes. A script link does nothing.
